SMS Terms of Service

The Law Office of J. Mario Gámez offers a text-messaging program to our clients for court-date and appointment reminders, case-status updates, and billing/payment notifications. You provide consent by completing and signing the firm's written SMS Consent Form, on which you provide your mobile number and mark a consent box that is unchecked by default; by signing that form you consent to receive these messages at the number provided. We text only clients who have signed this consent form.

Message frequency varies based on your case activity. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. For help, reply HELP or call (210) 222-0500. To stop, reply STOP at any time — you'll get a confirmation and no further messages. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of legal representation. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
